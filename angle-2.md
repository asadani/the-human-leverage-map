
Yes. In fact, I think the most interesting version is not a ladder but a  **2D map** , because two people can be at the same "AI maturity" level while having completely different emotional relationships with AI.

For example:

* A founder may deeply trust AI and delegate heavily.
* A security engineer may use AI extensively but trust it very little.

Both are sophisticated users.

A linear model loses that nuance.

## Level 0 — Ignore

Human does everything.

AI irrelevant.

Examples:

* "This is hype."
* "I'll wait."

---

## Level 1 — Consume

Uses AI as enhanced Google.

Examples:

* summaries
* questions
* search

AI = information source.

---

## Level 2 — Assist

Uses AI to accelerate execution.

Examples:

* coding
* writing
* presentations

AI = helper.

---

## Level 3 — Collaborate

Uses AI to improve thinking.

Examples:

* strategy discussions
* architecture reviews
* decision analysis

AI = thought partner.

This is where I would place many senior engineers and architects today.

---

## Level 4 — Orchestrate

Coordinates multiple AI systems.

Examples:

* agents
* workflows
* automation chains

AI = workforce.

Human = manager.

---

## Level 5 — Multiply

Creates organizations that scale via AI.

Examples:

* AI-first startups
* AI-native teams
* AI-powered products

Human = architect.

AI = infrastructure.

---

## Level 6 — Civilize

Shapes how society uses intelligence.

Examples:

* governance
* ethics
* education
* public infrastructure

Human = steward.

AI = societal force.
