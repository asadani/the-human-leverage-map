## The Human Leverage Map

Every chapter in this book refers back to the same two-axis diagram. Here it is now, in its simplest form.

```
                        THE HUMAN LEVERAGE MAP
                        ──────────────────────

  H I G H E R   ↑ Human Leverage
  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stewardship    │                          │  The Amplifier /
  Multiplication │                          │  Steward of Intelligence
  Leadership     │  The Wise Expert         │
  Judgment       │                          │
                 ├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────
  Expertise      │                          │
  Execution      │  The Resistant           │  The Prompt Addict
  Productivity   │                          │
  Survival       │                          │
  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
              L O W E R    ──────────────────────    H I G H E R

                           AI Capability →
              (Ignore → Consumer → Practitioner → Collaborator
                          → Builder → Orchestrator → AI-Native)

  ↑ Human Leverage
  (Survival → Productivity → Execution → Expertise
        → Judgment → Leadership → Multiplication → Stewardship)

  ● Hidden reading: Cognitive Sovereignty
    (the quality that separates the upper half from the lower)
```

**The vertical axis — Human Leverage** — measures what you bring that AI cannot replicate on demand: judgment, taste, relationships, accountability, the ability to ask the right question, and ultimately the wisdom to decide what to build and why.

**The horizontal axis — AI Capability** — measures how fluently you work with AI tools: from ignoring them entirely, through casual consumption and skilled practice, toward building, orchestrating, and designing systems where humans and AI operate as a single unit.

**The hidden reading is Cognitive Sovereignty**: the degree to which you remain the author of your own thinking, rather than a downstream consumer of someone else's model. It lives in the vertical axis but is not reducible to any single level on it. You will recognize it in the chapters ahead.

The four personas are not types of people. They are positions — places on the map that each of us can occupy, leave, and return to depending on the domain, the moment, and the choices we make.

## Locate Yourself

Three prompts. No scoring. No categories. Just a dot and an arrow.

**Prompt 1 — Where are you on AI Capability?**

Pick the level that honestly describes your current everyday practice — not your aspirations.

- **Ignore** — AI tools are not part of your workflow.
- **Consumer** — You use AI products as an end user, without deliberate prompting craft.
- **Practitioner** — You prompt with intention, iterate, and know when AI helps and when it doesn't.
- **Collaborator** — You treat AI as a working partner and design your prompts the way you'd write a brief.
- **Builder** — You build with AI: pipelines, automations, tools, products.
- **Orchestrator** — You design systems where AI components and humans work together, with deliberate handoffs and trust boundaries.
- **AI-Native** — Your practice is inseparable from AI. Your judgment operates at the level of what the system should do, not how to make a single call work.

Place a dot. The useful dot is the accurate one.

**Prompt 2 — Where are you on Human Leverage?**

This axis is subtler than seniority or years of experience. It is about what you are actually doing with your working time — what value you generate and why someone else cannot generate it in your place.

- **Survival** — Immediate, reactive demands consume your time. You execute what others define.
- **Productivity** — You complete work efficiently and reliably. Your value is throughput.
- **Execution** — You own deliverables end to end, taking a brief and producing a result.
- **Expertise** — Your judgment within your domain is a recognized asset. People come to you for the hard question, not just the task.
- **Judgment** — You make consequential calls under uncertainty. Your value is decision quality, not just knowledge.
- **Leadership** — You shape context for others: what gets worked on, how problems are framed, what success looks like.
- **Multiplication** — You increase the leverage of the people around you. Your presence makes others better.
- **Stewardship** — You hold responsibility for something larger than any team or deliverable: a direction, a standard, a future.

Place a second dot.

**Prompt 3 — Draw your vector.**

Now think about where you were roughly a year ago on each axis. Mark that point mentally, or jot it down. Draw an arrow from then to now. Is it pointing up the leverage axis, across the capability axis, or diagonally toward the upper-right?

Then ask one more question: where is the arrow pointing from where you stand today? That direction is more important than the dot.

The book's argument: the Scarcity Inversion is already underway. AI capability is becoming abundant and cheap. Human Leverage — especially the Cognitive Sovereignty that anchors the upper half of the map — is becoming the scarce resource. The people who will matter most are not those with the highest AI Capability score, nor those who have insulated themselves from AI entirely. They are in the upper-right: The Amplifier, the Steward of Intelligence, the practitioner who uses AI fluently without surrendering their thinking to it.

Hold your dot and your arrow in mind as you read. The map will keep coming back.

## Glossary

**The Scarcity Inversion** — The reversal in which AI commoditizes execution and prediction, making raw output abundant and cheap, while distinctly human capacities — judgment, accountability, wisdom, context — become the scarcer and therefore more valuable resource.

**The Human Leverage Map** — The two-axis framework at the center of this book: AI Capability on the horizontal, Human Leverage on the vertical, with four quadrant personas marking the positions that matter most.

**Cognitive Sovereignty** — The quality of remaining the author of your own thinking: the capacity to reason independently, hold ambiguity, exercise judgment, and resist the pull of algorithmic substitution even when substitution is available and easy.

**Steward of Intelligence** — The persona in the upper-right quadrant of the map: a leader or practitioner who commands AI capability fluently while operating at the level of judgment, accountability, and purpose that AI cannot replicate. Leverage that survives AI.
