## What the chapter establishes (mapped to brief)
1. **Historical arc** — Three concrete beats before AI: industrial/muscle (loom, steam, assembly line, the weaver), computing/calculation ("computer" as a job title, ballistics tables), internet-search/recall (the lawyer-from-memory, the moat of retrieval). Each beat shows the same consolation: machine took the lower faculty, human value moved up a level.
2. **AI's qualitative departure** — Framed as the pattern breaking: earlier faculties were *peripheral* to knowledge work (scaffolding around thinking); generative AI automates the core output itself — reasoning, drafting, synthesizing, coding. The "move up a level, the machine can't follow" escape route is named and shown to fail here.
3. **The turn** — "How do you feel about AI?" (fear vs. hype) reframed as the wrong, perishable question, supported by the electricity/internet/smartphone emotional cycle. Replaced with the durable structural question: what becomes scarce, where does value go? Deliberately left UNRESOLVED and handed to Chapter 2.
