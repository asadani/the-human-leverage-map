# The Human Leverage Map — Design Spec

**Date:** 2026-06-22
**Type:** Small book / long whitepaper (~8,000–20,000 words)
**Audience:** Leaders & practitioners (senior engineers, founders, product/leadership)
**Register:** Mental-model register (Maslow / Dreyfus / Naval lineage). Rigorous but readable; citations as credibility ballast, not academic apparatus.
**Decision:** ONE integrated work — the accessible Map as the spine, the cognition-mechanics research as the cited, load-bearing foundation.

---

## 1. The Through-Line (the single claim the book defends)

> When intelligence becomes abundant, judgment becomes scarce — and the same AI that grants you leverage can quietly erode the faculty that leverage depends on.

## 2. The Argument Chain — *The Scarcity Inversion*

1. Every epoch automated a human faculty: muscle → calculation → information → now **execution and expertise**.
2. AI commoditizes intelligence itself — the thing knowledge workers sold.
3. Scarcity *inverts*: value drifts to **judgment, taste, trust, responsibility, meaning**.
4. "Leverage" = the value you create that *survives* this commoditization.
5. But the tool that lifts you can hollow you (cognitive offloading → atrophy). Leverage is therefore **conditional**.
6. The map's top-right is not a destination — it is a posture that must be actively **defended**.

## 3. The Map (canonical structure)

The spine is **angle-1** (AI Capability × Human Leverage × Purpose), with **angle-2** folded in (its Fear→Stewardship "Relationship" line is demoted from a primary axis to a sub-theme, per the research's own warning that emotional axes age poorly). The mechanistic reports become the Cognitive Sovereignty layer.

```
                        HUMAN LEVERAGE  (value that survives AI)
                              ↑
        The Wise Expert       │      The Amplifier / Steward
        (low AI, high         │      (high AI, high leverage —
         durable value)       │       the defended summit)
                              │
        ──────────────────────┼──────────────────────→  AI CAPABILITY
                              │                          (leverage mode)
        The Resistant         │      The Prompt Addict
        (ignores AI,          │      (high AI, low durable
         replaceable)         │       value — busy, hollow)
                              ↓
```

- **X — AI Capability (leverage mode):** Ignore → Consumer → Practitioner → Collaborator → Builder → Orchestrator → AI-Native
- **Y — Human Leverage (value that survives AI):** Survival → Productivity → Execution → Expertise → **Judgment → Leadership → Multiplication → Stewardship**
- **Z (altitude) — Purpose / Scope:** Self → Team → Org → Industry → Society → Humanity (carries the "clickbait-AI vs. AI-for-education" distinction)
- **Hidden 4th reading — Cognitive Sovereignty:** not a position but a structural-integrity check (ascending vs. hollowing out). This is what the Mechanics section governs and what makes the framework original: no leverage model accounts for the atrophy failure mode; no atrophy research accounts for the leverage upside.

**Movement is expressed as vectors, not labels:** "Where was I last year, where am I now, which way am I pointed?" People can move in any direction, not just diagonally.

**Quadrant personas (kept as-is — punchy, fit the leaders register):** The Resistant, The Prompt Addict, The Wise Expert, The Amplifier / Steward of Intelligence.

## 4. Original Coinages (introduced as new; never falsely cited)

- **The Scarcity Inversion** — the macro-thesis.
- **The Human Leverage Map** — the framework / title.
- **Cognitive Sovereignty** — the faculty the summit must defend.
- Supporting: "leverage that survives AI" (durable value); "Steward of Intelligence" (the top-right posture).

## 5. Structure — 3 parts, ~11 short chapters + a self-assessment opener

**Front matter — "Locate yourself":** a one-page self-assessment so a leader can place their current position and vector before reading. (Kept in — adds practical value for this audience.)

**Part I · The Inversion (why)**
1. The faculty AI automates now — historical arc (muscle → calc → info → execution/expertise); why "how you feel about AI" is the wrong, perishable question.
2. The Scarcity Inversion — what becomes scarce when intelligence is abundant; the economics (prediction cheap → judgment dear).

**Part II · The Map (what)**
3. Why a map, not a ladder — 2D over maturity models; people don't move in one direction.
4. The X-axis — AI Capability / leverage mode (levels + examples).
5. The Y-axis — Human Leverage / value that survives AI.
6. The four quadrants + movement vectors (the personas).
7. Altitude — the Z-axis of Purpose / Scope.

**Part III · The Defense (how / risk)**
8. Cognitive Sovereignty — distributed cognition, System 0, cognitive offloading, Epistemic Confinement.
9. How leverage collapses — Captive of Autonomy, algorithmic monoculture, the illusion of competence (failure transitions).
10. Defending the summit — deliberate friction, formulation of intent, taste/discernment, verification & trust (practices for individuals, orgs, builders).
11. Coda — The Steward of Intelligence: the scarcest resource is judgment, and eventually wisdom.

## 6. Citation-Integrity Plan (a stated goal: trustworthiness)

The research files cite real-sounding sources, but several load-bearing claims (e.g., "95% of users offload Tier-1 sense-making," "17% Epistemic Confinement," specific arXiv IDs and 2026 journal entries) will **not** be carried over on trust. Rules for the book:

- **Original framework** (the map, the coinages) → labeled as ours; **no** citation. New thinking does not borrow authority.
- **Borrowed concepts** (extended mind, distributed cognition, Kahneman's System 1/2, Dreyfus model, *Prediction Machines* economics) → cited to canonical, verifiable sources.
- **Empirical claims** (cognitive-atrophy studies, GPS/hippocampus spatial memory, taste-vs-execution) → each **verified against a real, locatable source** (web search) before inclusion. Anything unverifiable is dropped or reframed as illustration — never given a fabricated citation.
- **Citation style:** endnotes (credibility ballast), not inline academic apparatus.

## 7. Non-Goals (YAGNI)

- No Hawkins-style numeric calibration scale (the research explicitly rejects false precision).
- No emotional primary axis (Fear→Love); emotion appears only as a sub-theme.
- Not two separate documents; not a purely academic whitepaper; not a short essay.

## 8. Open Defaults (decided in absence of further input)

- Persona names kept punchy ("Prompt Addict," "The Resistant") rather than neutralized.
- Self-assessment opener kept in.

Both are reversible during drafting if they read wrong.
